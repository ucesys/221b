Step 1 · Add a member, from a user's details or a group on the Roles page

Add to a local group

LDAP people get their groups from the directory and do not appear here.
The groups, their role and their namespaces are defined in auth.local.groups. To give a different set of namespaces, add a group there and run helm upgrade. A group may have one member.
After this, bob can
Adminincheckout, web through checkout-ops
Viewerinweb through web-readers (adds nothing: Admin already reads web)
Plus the nodes, for everyone who signs in.
This changes 221B, not the cluster. The bindings for the group already exist from Helm.
Step 2 · A confirmation, then the audit record

Add bob to web-readers?

bob will be a Viewer in web, on every cluster that has the group's bindings. The change applies within 2 minutes, while bob is signed in.

Nothing is written to Kubernetes. The change is kept by 221B and recorded in the audit log with your name.
The audit record 
{"time":"2026-10-16T12:11:04Z","actor":"mikhail@stolpner.com",
 "action":"group.member.add","user":"221b-local:bob",
 "group":"221b-local:web-readers","result":"ok"}
No second person is asked for a group of an Admin or Viewer role. Super roles may ask for one on a production cluster (open question).
