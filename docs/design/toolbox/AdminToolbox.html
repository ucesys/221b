Everything on this page is a ceiling. A person also needs the Kubernetes right (pods/exec, pods/ephemeralcontainers, jobs), decided by impersonation, and on a production connection a second SuperAdmin. The console sends only the fixed commands shown in Sessions, with their arguments escaped; it never widens its own limits, whatever an assistant suggests.
