# Code of Conduct

221B follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is not a substitute for it.

## What we expect

We want taking part in 221B to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Disagree about ideas, not people; accept a decision once it is made.
- Give and take feedback on the work, constructively.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

Not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults or personal attacks, harassment in public or in private, publishing someone's private information without their permission, and anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainers at [info@ucesys.com](mailto:info@ucesys.com). Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainers decide on the response, from a private warning to a permanent ban, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, and they respect the privacy and safety of whoever reported it.
